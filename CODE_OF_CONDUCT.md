# Code of conduct

## Our standard

BattCycle welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and experience level.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- discussing evidence and tradeoffs clearly
- giving actionable, specific review feedback
- respecting safety limits and user consent
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them
- protecting private vulnerability reports and personal data

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- publishing another person's private information
- encouraging unsafe battery use or bypassing safeguards without a responsible test plan
- using project spaces for spam or unrelated promotion
- retaliating against good-faith bug or security reports

## Enforcement

Open a private report through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/AlfWuxy/BattCycle/security/advisories/new) when public discussion could expose someone or reveal sensitive information. For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should be proportional, documented privately when appropriate, and focused on protecting the community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository spaces and when someone represents BattCycle in public.
